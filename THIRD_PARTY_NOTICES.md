## HarfBuzz

syllable machine (`tools/unicode/build_indic.py`, `tools/unicode/build_indic_machine.py`). Other shaping
behaviour (ZWJ handling in contexts, mark and cursive offsets) follows HarfBuzz's. From HarfBuzz 15.1.0,
